Key point

  • The papers of the “Vision and generative models” group in order of first public year, with the confirmed citations among them drawn as lines.
  • 33 papers (first public 1962–2021), 103 citation lines within the group; 22 of the 33 were first made public in 2012 or later.
  • Citations to papers of other groups are not drawn on this page.
