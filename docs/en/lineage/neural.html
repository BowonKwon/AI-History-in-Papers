Neural network learning

From formal neurons to perceptrons, backpropagation, convolutional and recurrent networks, normalization and residual connections.

Key point

  • The papers of the “Neural network learning” group in order of first public year, with the confirmed citations among them drawn as lines.
  • 39 papers (first public 1943–2017), 71 citation lines within the group; 10 of the 39 were first made public in 2012 or later.
  • Citations to papers of other groups are not drawn on this page.
